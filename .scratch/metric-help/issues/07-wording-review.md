## Comments

- 2026-10-08: The review page is [review/wording-review.md](../review/wording-review.md), generated by [review/make_review.py](../review/make_review.py) from the app's own components (the layout and one recompute of the latest Build's first dataset at default settings) and the User Guide; nothing is retyped. It is grouped by surface (summary row, Cluster quality, Inspector, grid headers, analytical controls, legend, dataset summary and Export), each with an approval box. Every row gives the element, its tooltip as the app builds it, the entry a click opens and the In short's word count; a tooltip is written out once and later elements with the same entry refer back to it. An appendix gives every linked entry's *Why it matters here.* paragraph (54 entries). Two open decisions head the page: "successful games" or "the most-played games" (the page lists the five entries that still say "success…"), and whether a pill's click should open the entry instead of its popover. Corrections go into the guide; rerun the script to refresh the page.
